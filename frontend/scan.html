1. Scan

How to scan a room

  1. Start, then sweep the floor slowly until a green ring appears.
  2. Each wall: tap its 4 corners as the picture shows. Green ring on the floor, white dot at the ceiling. Corner hidden? Tap beside it, or on the wall (blue ring). No ceiling in view? Skip.
  3. Next wall, turn right. After wall 4: Close room.
  4. Door / Window: 2 corners. Furniture: pick it, tap once. Then Finish.
  1. Move the phone slowly over the floor for a few seconds, then wait for the green ring.
  2. Aim the ring at a point and tap the camera view.
  3. Aim at the next point and tap again. A line and its length appear.
  4. Tap the chip that matches the real length of that line.

4. Results

Type the real length of any line into its Tape box, in cm. Clear the box to remove it.
